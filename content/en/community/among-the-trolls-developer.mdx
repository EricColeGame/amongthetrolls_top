## Who Is Avantgarden SRL, the Among the Trolls Developer?

Avantgarden SRL is the only studio named in the developer field on the store page. The "SRL" suffix is a company designation commonly used in Romania, which points toward a European independent team rather than an in-house studio owned by a large publisher.

That distinction shapes expectations. Independent teams typically ship a focused single-player experience first and expand later, and the Among the Trolls developer credit reflects exactly that: one protagonist, one central mystery, and no multiplayer at launch.

The studio's creative bet is Finnish folklore. Players step into Anna's shoes as she travels from the U.S. to a remote Finnish cabin to visit her grandfather — only to discover the cabin abandoned and her grandfather missing. From there, the story draws on the Kalevala and Finnish folk tradition rather than on generic fantasy trappings.

### What those design choices look like in play

- A single-player, first-person perspective that keeps the forest claustrophobic and personal
- Survival pressure from harsh weather, wildlife, and scarce resources layered over narrative beats
- Two interlocking systems — Väki (magic) and Lykky (luck) — that players balance rather than max out
- Crafting and building used as progression tools instead of a separate sandbox mode
- Mythological encounters rooted in classic Kalevala stories rather than standard monster archetypes

## FAQ

**Who is the Among the Trolls developer?**
Avantgarden SRL is the studio credited as developer on the Steam store page. Publishing is handled by 505 Pulse and 505 Games.

**Is Among the Trolls multiplayer or co-op?**
No. The store page lists single-player as a core feature, and there is no co-op or online multiplayer mode. It is a solo, first-person narrative adventure with survival elements.

**What is the game actually about?**
You play as Anna, who travels from the U.S. to a remote Finnish cabin to visit her grandfather, only to find the cabin abandoned and him missing. The story unfolds through Finnish mythology, shamanistic magic, and long-buried family history.

**Is it worth buying right now?**
That depends on what you value. Early community reports praise the atmosphere, mythology, and presentation while flagging difficulty and polish. With the introductory discount running through October 28 and a small review sample, waiting a few weeks for patches is a reasonable strategy if you are on the fence.

**Does the Among the Trolls developer plan post-launch content?**
