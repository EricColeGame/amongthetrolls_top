## What Is Among the Trolls? The Story in a Nutshell

According to the official Steam store listing, Among the Trolls is a first-person narrative-adventure game where the secret mysteries of Finnish forests are unraveled. You play as Anna, and the core loop blends story-driven investigation with survival pressure. The cabin is empty, the grandfather is gone, and the trail leads into folklore rather than logic.

The game draws heavily on the Kalevala, the classic Finnish epic, and on shamanistic magic traditions. Creatures you meet are not generic fantasy monsters — they are rooted in Nordic myth, and the world itself behaves as though nature has a will of its own. That animistic framing is what separates Among the Trolls from the crowded field of survival crafters.

## Among the Trolls First Look: Core Gameplay Systems

This Among the Trolls first look would be incomplete without the systems, because the survival layer is what gives the story its tension. ### Väki and Lykky: The Two Forces You Balance

The most distinctive mechanic is the balance between Väki (magic) and Lykky (luck). Rather than a single stamina bar or hunger meter, the game asks you to manage two ancient forces that reportedly intertwine. Väki is tied to magic and the supernatural side of the world; Lykky governs fortune and, presumably, how badly the wilderness wants to ruin your day.

| System | What It Covers | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Väki (Magic) | Supernatural ability and mythic interaction | Gates how you handle folklore encounters |
| Lykky (Luck) | Fortune, outcomes, survival odds | Shapes how the wild treats you |
| Weather | Harsh conditions and exposure | Forces planning around the seasons |
| Wildlife | Predators and scarce resources | Makes every trip outside the cabin risky |
| Crafting & Building | Tools, shelter, adaptation | Your main progression path |

### Survival, Crafting, and Archery

You will build, craft, and adapt to the environment as you unlock the secrets of your ancestors — that is straight from the developer's own description. Archery appears in the Steam tags, so ranged combat is part of the toolkit, though the game is framed as narrative-first rather than combat-first. Exploration, not extermination, is the point.

Practically, that means your first hours will look like this: scout the area around the cabin, gather what you can carry, establish a safe base, and only then push deeper into the forest where the mythology gets louder.

## Tips for Your First Hours in the Finnish Wilderness

Based on the systems described, here is how to start strong:

- **Secure the cabin area first.** Treat the abandoned cabin as your anchor point before wandering. It gives you a reference, a storage spot, and a reason to learn the local terrain.
- **Stockpile before you explore.** Scarce resources plus harsh weather is a bad combination. Gather aggressively in good conditions so you can stay put during bad ones.
- **Watch your Väki and Lykky balance.** These two forces are described as intertwined, so a heavy investment in one may change how the other behaves.
- **Do not ignore archery.** It appears as a core tag, and a ranged option is usually the safest answer to wildlife.
- **Read the lore.** The game is tagged Lore-Rich and Story Rich — environmental storytelling is doing real work here.
- **Play on adjustable difficulty if you are story-first.** There is no shame in softening survival pressure to enjoy the narrative.

## FAQ

**Is Among the Trolls a horror game?**
Not in the traditional jump-scare sense. It is a first-person narrative adventure with supernatural elements drawn from Finnish mythology. The tension comes from atmosphere, survival pressure, and the unknown rather than gore.

**When did Among the Trolls release and how much does it cost?**
It launched on October 14, 2026, at $19.99, with a 15% introductory discount bringing it to $16.99 through October 28. A soundtrack DLC is sold separately for $3.99.

**How long is Among the Trolls?**
The developer has not published an official completion time, and community reports at launch are still limited. Given the exploration and crafting focus, expect a story-driven campaign with optional wandering rather than a short linear ride.

**Does Among the Trolls support Steam Deck?**
Steam currently lists Steam Deck compatibility as Unknown. Check recent player reports before purchasing if handheld play is your main plan.

**Do I need to know Finnish mythology to enjoy it?**
No. The Kalevala-inspired creatures and shamanistic magic are woven into the story, and the game is designed to introduce them as Anna discovers her grandfather's world. Curiosity is enough.
