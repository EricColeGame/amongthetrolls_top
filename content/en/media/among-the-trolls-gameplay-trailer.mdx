### Väki and Lykky: the two meters that matter

The most distinctive system mentioned on the store page is the balance between Väki (magic) and Lykky (luck). Rather than a single health-and-hunger loop, the game frames survival around two ancient forces that apparently intertwine. In practice, that suggests you will be managing when to lean on supernatural power and when to trust fortune.

For new players, the practical takeaway is simple: watch how the trailer handles tension. If the footage shows a lot of retreating, hiding, and resource counting rather than constant combat, plan for a slower, more deliberate playstyle.

### Combat and archery

The game's community tags include archery and action, which points to ranged combat as a real part of the toolkit. That fits the survival framing — a bow is quiet, ammo is craftable, and it keeps the camera in first person. Do not expect a shooter. Expect a defensive tool that occasionally becomes a lifeline.

### Story delivery

Because this is a narrative-adventure first, expect dialogue, environmental storytelling, and collectible lore to carry as much weight as any fight. The "Story Rich" and "Lore-Rich" tags are doing honest work here.
