## Is There Multiplayer in Among the Trolls? The Straight Answer

Searching for **Among the Trolls multiplayer** options usually ends in the same place: the [official Steam store page for Among the Trolls](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1675260/Among_the_Trolls/), where the feature list says "Single-player" and stops there. The Finnish-mythology survival adventure from developer Avantgarden SRL launched on October 14, 2026, and it was designed as a solo, first-person story. In short, **Among the Trolls multiplayer** — online co-op, split-screen, or LAN — is not part of the package.

That catches a lot of people off guard. Survival-crafting games have trained players to expect a co-op mode, and trailers full of base building and forest exploration make it easy to assume you will be doing that with friends. Among the Trolls breaks the pattern. It is a narrative adventure first, and its tension depends on you being alone in the woods with nothing but your grandfather's notes and a very unfriendly wilderness.

## Why Among the Trolls Was Built for One Player

Among the Trolls puts you in Anna's shoes. She travels from the United States to Finland to visit her grandfather at a remote cabin deep in the forest, only to find the cabin abandoned and her grandfather gone. From there, the game pulls you into Nordic mythology, half-remembered summers, and a mystery that is deliberately personal.

That personal framing is exactly why co-op was not included. A story about family secrets and a vanished relative works best when there is no second player cracking jokes over voice chat. The design pillars on the store page reinforce it:

- **A missing-person mystery.** The core loop is investigation, not raid content. You follow clues left behind by a specific character in a specific place.
- **Two survival currencies.** You balance Väki (magic) and Lykky (luck) while dealing with harsh weather, wild animals, and scarce resources.
- **Finnish folklore creatures.** Encounters draw on the Kalevala and shamanistic tradition rather than generic fantasy monsters.
- **Crafting and shelter building.** You gather, build, and adapt to the environment as a solo survivor, not as a squad member.

Community reports from early players suggest the atmosphere is the main draw, which is another reason a lone-player design makes sense. The game currently sits at a "Mixed" rating on Steam across a small pool of reviews, with roughly 42% positive at the time of writing — a normal pattern for a niche narrative-survival hybrid in its first days.

## FAQ

**Does Among the Trolls have multiplayer?**
No. The official store listing shows single-player as the only gameplay feature, with no online co-op, split-screen, or LAN support. Any **Among the Trolls multiplayer** mode would have to be added by the developer in a future update, and nothing like that has been announced on the store page.

**Can I play Among the Trolls with a friend through Steam Family Sharing?**
You can share the game library, but that only lets each person play their own separate save. It does not create a co-op session, and you cannot play the same copy at the same time.

**Will Among the Trolls get multiplayer later?**
There is no confirmed roadmap for it. The game was pitched and built as a first-person narrative adventure about a solo journey into the Finnish wilderness, so a co-op patch would be a significant design change rather than a small addition. Watch the official Steam announcements page for any updates.

**Is Among the Trolls available on consoles or Steam Deck?**
At launch it is a Windows PC release on Steam only, with no console versions listed. Steam Deck compatibility is marked "Unknown," so verify the badge on the store page before assuming it runs well on a handheld.
