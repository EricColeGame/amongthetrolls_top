## What Is Among the Trolls? A Quick Primer

Among the Trolls is a first-person narrative-adventure game set deep in the forests of Finland, where the natural world and the supernatural bleed into one another. You venture into the wilderness to uncover family truths and confront what lies beyond, and the further you push into the trees, the more the forest seems to answer back.

The [official Among the Trolls website](https://amongthetrolls.com/) organizes the game around four pillars. They are worth understanding, because they tell you what kind of game you are actually waiting for:

- **Find Your Roots** — An empowering personal journey built around solving your grandfather's disappearance and the hidden truths that reshape your destiny.
- **Withstand the Natural and the Supernatural** — Harsh weather, wild animals, scarce resources, and encounters that do not obey ordinary rules.
- **Finnish Mythology** — A world drawn from Finnish folklore and ancient shamanistic magic, populated by creatures rooted in the classic Kalevala myths.
- **Survive the Wild** — Building, crafting, and adapting to an environment that is beautiful and unforgiving in equal measure.
