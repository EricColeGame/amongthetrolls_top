## What to Expect Once You're In the Forest

Community discussion and developer logs point to a game that punishes careless exploration. The following tips are drawn from player experience and the systems the developers have described — not from a finished retail build.

- **Balance magic and luck early.** Väki and Lykky appear to interact, so investing in one without the other may leave you exposed.
- **Respect the weather.** Storms and cold are framed as active threats, not background scenery.
- **Build before you wander.** Setting up a base near reliable resources reduces the risk of long, unprepared treks.
- **Treat sound as a warning system.** Supernatural encounters are often signaled before they're seen.
- **Expect scarcity.** Resource pressure is a core design pillar, so hoarding cautiously beats spending freely.
- **Don't ignore the story beats.** This is a narrative-adventure first; rushing past discoveries can lock you out of context.
