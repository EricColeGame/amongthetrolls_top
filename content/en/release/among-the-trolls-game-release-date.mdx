## What Kind of Game Is Among the Trolls?

Among the Trolls casts you as Anna, who travels from the United States to Finland to visit her grandfather at a remote cabin deep in the forest. She arrives to find the cabin abandoned and her grandfather missing, which pulls her into Nordic mythology and half-remembered summers from her past.

The result is a game that mixes narrative discovery with genuine survival pressure. You are not just reading lore — you are managing hunger, weather, wildlife, and scarce resources while piecing together what happened. Two systems sit at the center of that loop: Väki, tied to magic, and Lykky, tied to luck.

The developers lean heavily on the Kalevala, Finland's national epic, for creature design and atmosphere. That gives the game a distinct identity compared with the usual Norse-flavored adventure.
