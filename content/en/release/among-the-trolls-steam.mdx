## Why the Among the Trolls Steam Release Stands Out

Among the Trolls is developed by Avantgarden SRL and published by 505 Pulse and 505 Games. It is a first-person narrative-adventure game with survival elements, set in a remote corner of Finland where the forest is not scenery but a character with opinions.

You play as Anna, who travels from the United States to visit her grandfather in an isolated cabin deep in the woods. She arrives to find the cabin abandoned and her grandfather missing. That simple hook pulls her — and you — into a mystery built on family history, half-remembered summers, and the older layers of Finnish belief recorded in the Kalevala.

It is worth setting expectations early: despite the title, this is not a meme-troll game. The creatures and spirits here come from Finnish folklore and shamanistic tradition, and the game leans into atmosphere, exploration, and lore rather than jump-scare comedy.

## Gameplay Deep Dive: What You Actually Do in the Finnish Wilderness

The loop is familiar in shape and unusual in flavor. You explore a large, hand-built wilderness, gather and craft what you need, and manage your condition against harsh weather, wild animals, and scarce resources. Archery shows up among the popular tags, so expect ranged combat to matter.

What separates the experience is the magic system. Two resources drive it: Väki, tied to magic, and Lykky, tied to luck. Balancing them is presented as a core survival concern rather than a simple mana bar — the game frames them as ancient forces that intertwine, and the store page lists them prominently as part of its pitch.
